# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People preserving Gmail records offline, with Mike Dopp as the primary operator. They work on Windows with local or removable archives and need to search, inspect, and curate mail without depending on Google or internet access.

## Product Purpose

MailVault turns GYB backups and Google Takeout mail exports into an offline, searchable Gmail archive. Success means an operator can import an account's mail, retain evidence-quality headers, find messages and attachments, and perform reversible local cleanup with explicit destructive gates.

## Brand Personality

Direct, technically precise, and quietly confident. Status and evidence matter more than decoration.

## Anti-references

No marketing landing page, generic dashboard filler, decorative gradients, nested cards, hidden destructive behavior, or success claims that are not backed by an indexed artifact or runtime result.

## Design Principles

1. Put the archive workflow on the first screen.
2. Preserve source evidence and label inferred metadata.
3. Keep import, search, and cleanup states explicit.
4. Make local destructive actions reversible until the final confirmed purge.
5. Prefer durable receipts and counts over optimistic completion messages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-visible focus, semantic controls, readable contrast, non-color status cues, stable layouts, and reduced-motion support are required. Dynamic counts use tabular figures and errors name the failed file or dependency.
